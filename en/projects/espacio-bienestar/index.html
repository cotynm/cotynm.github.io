Branding

Brand identity for a wellness center

Naming and identity for Alma Alquimia, a center for therapies and holistic wellness in the hills of Córdoba.

Role
Brand designer
ARG
2021
Scope
Naming, identity and applications

The project

Creating a new space

Alma Alquimia is one of the many “reinventions” that came out of the 2020 pandemic: a hotel in the hills of Córdoba was to be remodeled to house holistic practices and training in alternative therapies and personal wellness.

Besides developing the brand identity, I advised on choosing the name, since the owner's first choice wasn't available for registration.

What were we looking for with the brand?

Giving the name presence

The design is built entirely around the name: two words, each with a powerful meaning, which we set out to represent through the symbol, the typefaces and the colors.

Conveying the brand's essence

We created a symbol inspired by alchemy's symbols: on one side, the soul and the divine (triangle); on the other, the human being (eye) and nature (drops, petals), and how they come together in a cyclical balance (circle).

The identity

Color palette and pattern

The palette has three colors: an ochre/gold, a light blue and a neutral dark gray.

The ochre/gold stands for the elements of the earth, the divine. The light blue, the ethereal, the spiritual. The dark gray, a neutral tone, brings groundedness, seriousness and trust.

Used thoughtfully, these three colors can produce very delicate, high-quality results.
