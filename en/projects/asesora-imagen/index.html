Personal brand design for an image consultant

Brand identity development for a professional image consulting business.

Role
Brand designer
ARG
2021
Scope
Identity, usage guide and applications

The project

Inspiration and sketches

Delicate and organic

The logo builds on a sober, elegant image, focusing on small details and on a reading hierarchy: initials, field, full name.

While it signals a distinctive, high-quality service, it also includes some resources so it works for a broader audience without losing the delicacy and details associated with good image in the field.

Color palette

Typefaces

A classic serif typeface was chosen to convey sobriety in the initials, contrasted with a sans serif for the rest, which adds a more modern, relaxed touch.

For supporting text, a third type family is suggested, with more variants and excellent legibility.
