Branding

Brand identity for a café

A modern, disruptive image for a new café in a town with deep-rooted traditions.

Role
Brand designer
ARG
2021
Scope
Logo, palette, icons and applications

The project

A corner and a dream

Like many people, these two partners left their jobs during the 2020 pandemic and bet everything on a traditional corner in a small town in Córdoba. A café-bakery facing the main square meant a very diverse audience to win over.

Inspiration and sketches

Memorable and friendly

The client wanted to represent the brand with a civet, a mammal whose droppings yield one of the most expensive coffees in the world.

We aimed to draw it so that it would be easy to remember and the place could be recognized through it. To tie it to the business, we added the subtle detail of a coffee cup handle.

Palette, icons and pattern

Although the logo and symbol are shown in black, we defined a color palette to support designs and pieces that need it, such as icons, patterns, etc.

It's made up of earthy colors, cheerful but desaturated.

The colors relate to the raw ingredients of the main products the place offers. Tan and green: coffee and the coffee plant; yellow: wheat.
