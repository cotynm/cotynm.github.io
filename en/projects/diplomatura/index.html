Branding

Brand identity for a university program

Naming and rebranding for the Diploma in E-commerce and Digital Marketing at the National University of Córdoba.

Role
Designer and communications lead
ARG
2018 – 2025
Scope
Identity, communication strategy and promotional pieces

The project

An identity with history

Since 2010 I've been part of this academic program, which started as a course/workshop for entrepreneurs and small businesses and has grown into a yearly diploma program, part of the offering of the Outreach Office of the School of Economics at the National University of Córdoba, Argentina.

As designer and head of communication and outreach (and a teacher), I developed its brand identity from the very beginning. Over the years, the logo changed a couple of times as the program evolved into new formats.

When it became a diploma program in 2018, we carried out a more thorough naming and rebranding process, since the program would gain more weight and required a new set of communication standards that needed work.

Challenges and requirements

  • Finding a name that was descriptive and representative was challenging, since the program covers a very broad syllabus across several disciplines.

    As a solution, together with the team we came up with a name that literally describes the two main subjects the program covers. To keep it simple and memorable, we also created an easy-to-pronounce acronym as a short name.

  • Creating a logo that could live in many formats, pieces and contexts, most of the time alongside other institutional or corporate logos.

    To address this, the design solution included a logo with alternatives and versions: logo with the full name, with acronym and full name, acronym only, and a symbol that can also be used on its own.

What were we looking for with the brand?

Conveying innovation

From the start, the program stood out for being aligned with the latest trends in e-commerce and digital sales. It was one of the first trainings on the subject in Córdoba and has always stayed up to date.

Reflecting action

The training offers a broad range of skills and tools in an integrated way, always focused on their practical application to each type of project. Core foundations and ways to apply them.

We achieved this by choosing the “on” switch icon, which stands for “activating” or launching a feature in digital products. It reflects the program's goal for its students: to launch their digital projects strategically and professionally.

The identity

Color palette

The color palette is limited, since the brand will live in many different contexts.

The main color, in the green/aqua range, was kept from previous identities, since it already set this program apart from other institutional offerings. The main palette also includes 2 neutrals, light gray and dark gray.

Each yearly cohort gets its own identity, with specific graphics, styles and strategies. That's why we created a secondary palette that can change from year to year.

Typography

Since the name is long, we chose a sans serif typeface to ensure legibility.

We also looked for a type family with alternative variants.
