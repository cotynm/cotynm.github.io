About me

I'm Constanza, but everyone calls me Coty. In every project, I like to get involved in the whole process: a person's work starts long before their task of the day, and its impact carries into the stages that follow. That's why I care about organization, coordination, empathy and respect for the people I work with.

My background is in graphic design and communication studies. After working in branding, copywriting, communication, e-commerce, web design and marketing, I moved into user experience and interface design (UX/UI).

What I enjoy most is listening, organizing and finding the clearest way to solve something.
